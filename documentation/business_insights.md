# Business Insights

## 1. Sales are concentrated in a few states

### Evidence
São Paulo (SP) has the highest sales with around **R$5.20M**, followed by Rio de Janeiro (RJ) with **R$1.82M** and Minas Gerais (MG) with **R$1.59M**.

### Business Implication
Sales are mostly concentrated in a few states, especially São Paulo. These states may need more attention for sales, inventory and logistics planning.

---

## 2. Repeat customers are a small part of the customer base

### Evidence
There are **2,997 repeat customers** out of **96,096 unique customers**, which gives a repeat-customer rate of **3.12%**.

### Business Implication
Only a small part of the customer base are repeat customers. This shows that customer retention and repeat purchases could be investigated further.

---

## 3. Repeat customers contribute more sales compared to their customer share

### Evidence
Repeat customers generated around **R$778,821.97**, which is **5.73% of total product sales**, while they represent only **3.12% of the customer base**.

### Business Implication
Repeat customers are a small part of the customer base but contribute a higher share of sales. This makes them an important customer group to understand.

---

## 4. Sales increased over time but there are monthly changes

### Evidence
The monthly sales trend shows strong growth from 2017 to 2018. Some months reached more than **R$800K** and some were close to **R$1M**.

### Business Implication
Sales increased over time, but there are also month-to-month changes. This can be useful for planning demand and sales activity.

### Data Limitation
September 2018 is only a partial month in the dataset, so the sharp drop in that month should not be treated as a normal full-month result.

---

## 5. Top sales categories are different from high-price categories

### Evidence
There was **no overlap between the Top 5 categories by total sales value and the Top 5 categories by average item price**.

For example, **computers** had a high average item price of **R$1,098.34**, while **health & beauty** had high total sales of around **R$1.26M**.

### Business Implication
The categories with the highest sales are not always the categories with the highest product prices. Sales performance is also related to volume and product mix.

---

## 6. Payment value is mainly concentrated in credit card and boleto

### Evidence
Credit card had around **R$12.54M** in payment value, followed by boleto with around **R$2.87M**. The other payment methods had much lower values.

### Business Implication
Most payment value comes from credit card and boleto, so these payment methods are important for the checkout and payment process.

---

## 7. Most customer reviews have a 5-star rating

### Evidence
There are **57,328 five-star reviews**, followed by **19,142 four-star reviews**, **8,179 three-star reviews**, **3,151 two-star reviews**, and **11,424 one-star reviews**.

### Business Implication
Five-star reviews are the largest group. However, there are still many low-rated reviews that could be investigated to understand customer problems.

---

## 8. Most delivered orders are on time, but some orders are not delivered

### Evidence
The overall **On-Time Delivery Rate is about 93.23%** for delivered orders with an actual delivery date. There are also **2,965 Not Delivered orders** and **6,535 Late orders**.

### Business Implication
Delivery performance is generally strong for delivered orders, but undelivered orders are a separate issue that should also be monitored.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Sales are mainly concentrated in São Paulo and a few other states.
- Repeat customers are a small part of the customer base but contribute a higher share of sales.
- High-sales categories are not always the categories with the highest average product prices.
- Credit card and boleto are the main payment methods by value.
- Most reviews are 5-star reviews, but low-rated reviews should still be investigated.
- Most delivered orders are on time, but some orders remain undelivered.
- Sales increased over time, but monthly sales also have fluctuations.
